Interior Design Cost in Hyderabad: Everything You Need to Know Before Starting

By Apple Interiors • May 15, 2024 • Pricing Guide

One of the most common questions we get at Apple Interiors is, "How much does interior design cost in Hyderabad?" The truth is, there's no one-size-fits-all answer. The cost depends on several factors including the size of your home, the quality of materials, and the complexity of the design. However, having a ballpark figure can help you plan your budget more effectively. Here's a comprehensive guide to interior design costs in Hyderabad for 2024.

1. Understanding the Cost Breakup

Interior design costs are typically divided into several categories: materials, labor, design fees, and taxes. In Hyderabad, material costs (wood, laminates, hardware) usually account for 60-70% of the total budget, while labor costs make up 20-25%.

2. Average Cost by Property Type

Here are some estimated starting prices for standard interior design in Hyderabad:

Property Type Basic (Lakhs) Premium (Lakhs) Luxury (Lakhs)
2BHK Flat 4.5 - 6.5 7.0 - 10.0 12.0+
3BHK Flat 6.5 - 9.0 10.0 - 15.0 18.0+

3. Factors Influencing the Cost

Several factors can push your interior design cost up or down:

  • Quality of Materials: Premium laminates, solid wood, and high-end hardware will significantly increase the cost.
  • Design Complexity: Intricate false ceilings, custom-made furniture, and detailed wall paneling require more labor and materials.
  • Scope of Work: Whether you're doing just the essential woodwork or a full home transformation including flooring, painting, and decor.
  • Location: While costs are fairly standard across Hyderabad, some areas like Jubilee Hills or Banjara Hills might have higher logistical or labor costs.

4. How to Save on Interior Design Costs

It's possible to create a stunning home without overspending. Focus your budget on high-usage areas like the kitchen and living room. Choose durable yet affordable materials for bedrooms. Avoid excessive ornamentation and stick to a clean, functional design.

5. Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

Always ask your designer about potential hidden costs like transportation, site cleaning, and GST. A transparent quotation should include all these elements to avoid surprises later.

Conclusion

Planning your interior design project with a clear understanding of costs will make the process much smoother.
