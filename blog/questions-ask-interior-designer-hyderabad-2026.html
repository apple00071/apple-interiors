7 Questions Every Hyderabad Homeowner Should Ask Before Hiring an Interior Designer

By Apple Interiors • April 13, 2026 • Design Advice

More Choices, More Confusion — Here Is How to Cut Through

Hyderabad's interior design market has never been more crowded. New residential launches in Kokapet, Tellapur, Narsingi, and Kompally are creating a wave of homeowners looking for reliable designers — and a wave of studios marketing themselves as the best. Before you sign anything or pay an advance, here are seven questions that will separate genuine experience from polished brochures.

  1. 1. Can I See Work From a Similar Property Type and Neighbourhood?

    A studio that primarily handles luxury villas in Jubilee Hills may not understand the constraints of a 2BHK in Miyapur — and vice versa. Ask to see portfolio work that matches your flat size, building type, and area. Light conditions, layout challenges, and storage needs vary significantly between Banjara Hills and Secunderabad, or between Kondapur and Malkajgiri.

  2. 2. Is This a Turnkey Project or Will I Manage Vendors Myself?

    Turnkey means the design firm handles everything — civil, electrical, carpentry, furniture, and final styling. Non-turnkey means you coordinate between a designer, a contractor, a carpenter, and multiple suppliers yourself. For most homeowners in Gachibowli, Manikonda, and Nallagandla buying their first home, turnkey reduces stress and ensures quality consistency. Confirm this upfront and get the scope in writing.

  3. 3. What Does the Per Square Foot Price Actually Include?

    In 2026, Hyderabad interior design costs typically range from ₹1,200 to ₹3,500 per sq. ft. depending on materials and scope. Always ask for a line-item cost breakup — not a lump sum. Watch for common hidden extras: transportation charges, labour supervision fees, finishing costs, and brand hardware mark-ups that get added after the initial quote is accepted.

  4. 4. Which Material Brands and Hardware Are You Specifying?

    The difference between a kitchen that lasts 3 years and one that lasts 15 comes down almost entirely to hardware and plywood quality. Ask specifically about plywood grade (MDF vs. BWR vs. marine ply), hardware brands (Hettich, Hafele, and Sleek are the benchmarks), and finish quality. Apple Interiors is a certified partner for Sleek, Hafele, Hettich, and Greenply — brands that carry manufacturer warranties independent of the designer.

  5. 5. What Is a Realistic Timeline for My Project?

    A 2BHK in Kukatpally or Miyapur typically takes 45–60 days from work order to handover. A full 3BHK in Kondapur or Financial District takes 60–90 days. Any designer promising significantly shorter timelines on a full-home project should be questioned carefully — rushed execution almost always shows in the quality of finish, particularly in joinery and paintwork.

  6. 6. What Warranty Do You Provide on Work and Materials?

    A reputable studio offers a minimum of 1 year on workmanship. Manufacturer warranties on Hettich and Hafele fittings extend up to 5 years independently. Always get warranty terms documented before signing. Ask specifically what happens if a shutter warps, a drawer rail fails, or a panel delaminates — who covers the cost and how quickly is it resolved?

  7. 7. Who Is My Point of Contact and How Is Site Supervision Done?

    Ask whether the lead designer who presents your concept is also managing your site — or whether it gets handed to a junior team after sign-off. For projects in Secunderabad, Uppal, LB Nagar, or Malkajgiri — which are further from most design studios concentrated in West Hyderabad — confirm how frequently site visits happen and how daily progress is communicated to you.
