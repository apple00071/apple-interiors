Frequently Asked Questions

Can my parents or relatives in Hyderabad visit your experience center?

Yes! Your local family members are always warmly welcome to visit our experience center in Kukatpally. We can walk them through finished modular kitchen displays, wardrobe mechanisms, and material samples while simultaneously having you on a video call.

How do milestone payments work for overseas clients?

We follow a transparent, stage-based payment plan. Payments are linked to verifiable deliverables (e.g. 3D sign-off, factory dispatch, civil/false ceiling completion, final installation). You can easily pay through your NRE/NRO accounts, wire transfers, or online payment gateways.

What brands of materials and hardware do you use?

We use only top-tier certified materials: CenturyPly, Greenply, Merino, and Greenlam for core woodwork, and world-class German hardware from Hettich and Hafele. Every piece carries an official manufacturer warranty of up to 10 years.

How do we hand over keys to your team in Hyderabad?

You can authorize your society management, builder handover desk, or local relatives to hand over the keys. We sign a formal custody acknowledgment, install secure digital locks during execution if desired, and hand back all keys upon final completion.
