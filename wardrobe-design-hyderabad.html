Materials & Finishes for Wardrobes

The durability and appearance of your wardrobe depend heavily on the materials used. At Apple Interiors, we only use high-quality materials suited for Hyderabad's humid climate.

Carcass (Frame) Materials

BWR Plywood

Boiling Water Resistant plywood – the standard choice for most wardrobes. Handles moderate humidity well and offers 10+ years of durability.

BWP Plywood

Boiling Water Proof plywood – ideal for ground floor apartments or areas with high humidity. Premium option for maximum durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about wardrobe design in Hyderabad.

Wardrobe costs vary depending on size, type, and finish. We provide a detailed, customized quote after a free site visit and measurement, ensuring you get the best value for your specific requirements.

Sliding wardrobes are better for compact bedrooms as they don't need door clearance space. Hinged wardrobes offer better visibility and easier access to contents. In Hyderabad's typical 10x12 bedrooms, sliding wardrobes are more popular due to space constraints. Hinged wardrobes work better in larger rooms or walk-in setups.

We use BWR or BWP grade plywood for the carcass (frame), ensuring durability in Hyderabad's humid climate. For shutters, options include laminate (most affordable), acrylic (glossy modern look), lacquered glass, PU finish (premium matte), and veneer (natural wood look). All hardware is from Hettich or Hafele with soft-close mechanisms.

A standard bedroom wardrobe takes 2-3 weeks from order to installation. This includes 3-5 days for measurement and design, 10-14 days for manufacturing at our factory, and 1-2 days for installation. Walk-in closets may take 3-4 weeks depending on complexity.

Our wardrobes include accessories like trouser pullouts, tie and belt racks, saree pullouts, shoe racks, jewelry drawers, lockable compartments, LED lighting, full-length mirrors, and laundry baskets. All accessories are customized based on your storage needs and lifestyle.
