Core Elements of Our Living Room Designs

When designing a living room in Hyderabad apartments, we focus on several critical elements that transform ordinary halls into extraordinary spaces. Our approach combines international design trends with practical considerations for Indian homes.

1. Custom TV Units That Define Your Space

The TV unit is often the focal point of modern living rooms. We design units that go beyond just holding your television – they become statement pieces. Our TV unit designs include floating units with LED backlighting, floor-to-ceiling wall units with extensive storage, units integrated with study areas or bar cabinets, and minimalist designs with hidden cable management. We use materials like PU finish, acrylic, back-painted glass, and veneer to match your style.

2. False Ceiling & Lighting Design

Proper ceiling design and lighting can completely transform the mood of your living room. We offer gypsum false ceilings (most popular in Hyderabad), POP designs for complex shapes, and combination ceilings with wood or metal accents. Our lighting solutions include cove lighting for ambient glow, spot lights for accent illumination, chandelier provisions for a touch of luxury, and smart lighting integration for modern homes.

3. Wall Treatments & Accent Walls

An accent wall can elevate your living room from ordinary to extraordinary. We specialize in wallpaper application (from subtle textures to bold patterns), textured paint finishes (Italian stucco, Korean coating), 3D wall panels and stone cladding, wooden panelling and fluted designs, and back-painted glass installations. Our designers help you choose the right treatment based on your room's lighting, size, and existing furniture.

4. Smart Partitions for Open Layouts

Many Hyderabad apartments have open living-dining layouts. Our partition designs create visual separation while maintaining the open feel. Options include CNC-cut MDF or plywood partitions with intricate patterns, frosted glass partitions for light flow, wooden slat dividers for a contemporary look, and bookshelf partitions that add storage.
